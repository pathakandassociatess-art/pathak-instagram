The Supreme Court recently held that a grandson has no right by birth in property that his father inherited from the grandfather under Section 8 of the Hindu Succession Act.

The court added that such property becomes the father's individual property and not joint family property, and therefore the grandson cannot claim a share in it by calling it "ancestral".

CASE : J. Rajesh (Minor) v. M. Kumaran (Dead) through LRs
