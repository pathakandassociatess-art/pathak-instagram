WHAT THE COURT HELD

The Verdict

1

Property inherited under Section 8 is the son's individual property, not joint family property.

2

His son gets no right by birth and cannot claim it as "ancestral".

3

Section 8 is a self-contained code. Section 6 does not apply here.

4

Following Chander Sen, the appeal was dismissed.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Not every grandfather's property is "ancestral". How and when it was inherited decides it.

A father can usually deal with such property as his own.
Check the date of death & mode of inheritance before any partition claim.
