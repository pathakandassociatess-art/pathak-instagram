The Supreme Court on 27 November found prima facie "wilful and flagrant disobedience" of its 20 November order on the Thiruparankundram (Madurai) Deepathoon lamp, with reports quoting the bench's remark of "administrative chaos" in Tamil Nadu. It directed that the lamp be lit at the Deepathoon on 28 November.

The court added that security of the temple and hill is handed to the CRPF, suspended the temple's board of trustees, and issued show-cause notices to officials and trustees on why contempt proceedings should not be initiated. The court said it would confine itself to the non-compliance, not the main dispute.

CASE : Thiruparankundram Deepathoon lamp-lighting contempt matter (Tamil Nadu)
CITATION : Supreme Court of India, 27 November 2026 – Bench of Justice Aravind Kumar & Justice Vipul M. Pancholi
