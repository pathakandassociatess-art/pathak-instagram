The Supreme Court on 26 November quashed the Bihar government's remission to former MP Anand Mohan, calling it "unsustainable in law", and ordered him to surrender within a month. It also struck down Bihar's 2023 amendment to its prison rules that had enabled his early release.

The court held that the family of the victim, here the widow of IAS officer G. Krishnaiah, has a right to be heard on remission. Bihar may consider remission afresh, but only after giving her a hearing.

CASE : Uma Krishnaiah's challenge to the early release of Anand Mohan (murder of IAS officer G. Krishnaiah) and Bihar's 2023 prison manual amendment
CITATION : Supreme Court of India, 26 November 2026 – Bench of Justice Dipankar Datta & Justice Sheel Nagu
