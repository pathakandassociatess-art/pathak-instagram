The Supreme Court on Wednesday set aside the Allahabad High Court's orders directing the CBI and ED to verify a disproportionate-assets complaint against Congress leader Rahul Gandhi, and sent the matter back to the High Court for fresh consideration.

The bench directed the petitioner to implead Gandhi as a respondent and said he must be heard afresh, including on the question of maintainability of the petition.

CASE : Rahul Gandhi's challenge to Allahabad High Court orders on a disproportionate-assets complaint filed by S. Vignesh Shishir
CITATION : Supreme Court of India, 25 Nov 2026, CJI Surya Kant, Justice Joymalya Bagchi & Justice V. Mohana
