The Supreme Court recently held that the Uttar Pradesh Qualifying Service for Pension and Validation Act, 2021 and the Uttar Pradesh Entitlement to Pension and Validation Act, 2025 are unconstitutional, as the State cannot use validating laws to override the Court's own Prem Singh judgment.

The court added that service in a work-charged establishment counts as qualifying service for pension and cannot be retrospectively excluded. It clarified that the State may still distinguish between modes of appointment and kinds of establishments, and directed that dues not paid in time carry 9% simple interest per annum.

CASE : Uttar Pradesh Ground Water Department Non Gazetted Employee Association (Regd.) & Anr. v. The Chief Secretary & Ors.
