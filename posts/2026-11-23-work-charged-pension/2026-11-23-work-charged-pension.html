THE CASE

Years of Service, Then Excluded

👷
Employees and retirees of the Public Works, Ground Water and Irrigation departments had served in work-charged establishments.
⚖️
In Prem Singh, the Supreme Court held such service counts as qualifying service for pension.
📜
UP then passed validation Acts in 2021 and 2025 to exclude that service.

WHAT THE COURT HELD

Laws Can't Override Judgments

Pre-regularisation service cannot be retrospectively excluded from pension by a validating law.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Work-charged service can count towards pension, and a State law can't simply wipe it out.

Keep appointment letters & service records of your work-charged years.
A rejected claim needs a hearing and a reasoned order.
It isn't automatic. It depends on your appointment and establishment type.
