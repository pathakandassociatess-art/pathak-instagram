The Supreme Court recently held that while increasing compensation in a motor accident case, a court must examine each head under which the Tribunal made its award and decide whether the amount granted was just.

The court added that a lump-sum increase "without indicating the basis" is guesswork rather than applying the law, and it raised the accident victim's total compensation to ₹11,75,106, with 7% interest.

CASE : Mohammad Sabir v. Ramesh Prasad & Ors.
