02 · WHAT HAPPENED

A Judge vs. The SP

📍
An Additional Sessions Judge in Rohtas (Sasaram), Bihar told the SP to register a case for allegedly false evidence.
⏳
When that wasn't done, he started contempt proceedings against the SP.
⚡
He summoned the SP and convicted him on the same day.

“Enough of adventure.”

— SUPREME COURT TO THE JUDGE
PATHAK & ASSOCIATESSUPREME COURT
03 · WHAT THE SUPREME COURT SAID

Power Has Limits

1
Disobeying a court direction is not automatically contempt "in the face of the court".
2
The High Court, not the sessions judge himself, should have dealt with it.
3
Summoning and convicting on the same day drew sharp objection from Justice Bagchi.
4
The judge was advised to undergo training at the National Judicial Academy.
PATHAK & ASSOCIATESKNOW THE LAW
KEY TAKEAWAY

Contempt power must be used with restraint, and through the right forum.

✓
Contempt of a subordinate court is for the High Court to decide (Contempt of Courts Act, 1971).
✓
Everyone, even a police officer, deserves a fair hearing before punishment.
