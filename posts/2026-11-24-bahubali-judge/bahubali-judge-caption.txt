The Supreme Court recently pulled up a Bihar Additional Sessions Judge from Rohtas (Sasaram) for summoning a Superintendent of Police and convicting him of contempt on the same day, telling the judge to "stop acting like Bahubali" and that it was "enough of adventure".

The court added that disobedience of a judicial direction is not automatically contempt committed in the face of the court, that the High Court should have dealt with the matter, and advised the judge to undergo further training at the National Judicial Academy.

CASE : Contempt conviction of SP by Rohtas Additional Sessions Judge (Bihar)
