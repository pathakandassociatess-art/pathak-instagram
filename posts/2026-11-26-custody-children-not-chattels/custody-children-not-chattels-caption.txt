The Supreme Court recently set aside a Madras High Court order that had given custody of two teenage sons to their father, holding that "children are not mere chattels, nor toys for their parents". The boys, now 16 and 14, have lived with their mother since 2014 and told the Court they wished to stay with her.

The court added that the welfare of the child is the primary consideration, and that the principle favouring the father as natural guardian is subject to exceptions and is not a universal rule. The father was allowed to meet the sons on the second Saturday and Sunday of every month at the Gandhi Museum, Madurai, and during Pongal and half-yearly vacations, and was told to share future educational expenses if asked.
