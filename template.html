Head-by-Head, Not Guesswork

A lump-sum increase without indicating the basis is guesswork, not applying the law.
Future loss of earnings (50% disability)₹7,52,556

Income taken at minimum wage (₹5,270/month) with 40% future prospects and a multiplier of 17, as no proof of income was produced.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Accident victims are entitled to "just compensation", calculated under every head, not a rough figure.

Keep medical bills & receipts. They were accepted in full.
Keep proof of income. Without it, courts fall back on minimum wage.
Disability, future prospects, pain & attendant costs are all separate heads.
